# Project: Nudge — Engage v2

## What Nudge Is

Nudge is a consumer personal finance app that helps people build better money habits. Users connect bank accounts, see where their money is going, set savings goals, and get nudges to stay on track.

- Launched 4 years ago
- Series B funded ($42M)
- 2.1 million registered users
- 340,000 monthly active users (MAU)
- Growing 28% YoY on MAU

## Current Phase

**Discovery** for Engage v2.

Nudge's acquisition funnel works well (signup → connect account → spending breakdown), but a large portion of users go passive after that initial "aha moment." 30-day retention dropped from 44% to 37% over the last two quarters.

Discovery will incorporate user feedback, competitive intelligence, and other inputs, with the goal of producing a working prototype to validate a solution direction with real users. Prototype format, timeline, and sign-off criteria are not yet decided (see [CLAUDE.md](CLAUDE.md) Open/TBD).
